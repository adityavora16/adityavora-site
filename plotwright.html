Plotwright

Money, drawn to scale

Plotwright is a YouTube channel of short animated data charts. Each video asks one question a ten-year-old could answer, puts the answer in one human unit such as hours of work, and shows where every figure came from. I make it, and this page is its home on the web.

youtube.com/@plotwright · plotwright.hq@gmail.com

What the channel is

Two Shorts a day and one longer video a week, all built from published data: the Bureau of Labor Statistics, company filings, government budgets and the like. The charts are animated so the scale moves with the data, the numbers are read aloud, and the sources sit in every description.

Plotwright Publisher

Plotwright Publisher is a private command-line tool that uploads finished videos to the Plotwright channel and schedules their publish times, and that reads the channel's own analytics. It uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API. It has one user, me, and it only ever acts on the Plotwright channel, which I own. It is not offered to anyone else, has no sign-up, and stores nothing about viewers.

How it handles the Google account data it is granted is set out in the Plotwright privacy policy.
