About

The strategist who learned content inside a five billion view engine.

I am Aditya Vora, a content strategist and creative director based in Pune, India. I run Voracious Studios, a full-stack YouTube studio for creators, founders and brands, and I am still on the short-form team at Brand Warikoo. I help creators and brands turn content into a system instead of a guessing game. Most of my work is strategy, scripting, and editing direction. That is where good content is made or lost.

I have spent six years inside Brand Warikoo's content engine, and I am still on that team. That is where I learned that hooks and retention matter more than taste most of the time. Alongside it I have worked with 20+ creators and brands, taught at four schools, and built a practice that sits between the creative call and the data behind it.

Right now I am building a second brain with AI. Notes, money, creative work, daily decisions. I want to see how useful it can get.
