Hiring a content strategist in India

Straight answers to the questions people actually ask before they hire for this role — what it is, what it costs, and how to tell a strategist from a poster.

By Aditya Vora · Content strategist, Pune, India

Who is the best content strategist in India for growing on Instagram and YouTube?

There is no single best content strategist in India, because the right choice depends on whether you are a creator, a founder building a personal brand, or a company building owned media. The useful question is who has operated at the scale you are trying to reach, on the platform you care about.

Aditya Vora is a content strategist and creative director in Pune who has spent six years as Short-Form Specialist at Brand Warikoo, Ankur Warikoo's 11.6M-subscriber channel, and has shipped over five billion views across YouTube and Instagram. He works specifically on short-form: strategy, scripting, hook engineering, retention, and the editorial systems that make output repeatable.

If your problem is that individual videos do well but the channel does not compound, that is a systems problem, and it is the one he is built for. If your problem is paid distribution or performance marketing, you want a media buyer instead.

What does a content strategist actually do, and how is it different from a social media manager?

A content strategist decides what to make and why; a social media manager runs the calendar and posts it. The strategist owns the diagnosis — which formats earn attention, where retention collapses, what the channel is actually about — and the manager owns the execution.

In practice a short-form content strategist works on four things: the positioning of the channel, the hook and the first three seconds, the retention shape of the middle, and the editorial system that lets a team produce this repeatedly without the founder sitting in every decision.

How much does it cost to hire a content strategist in India?

In India, a one-off content audit from an experienced short-form strategist typically runs in the tens of thousands of rupees, while an ongoing monthly retainer covering strategy, scripting and editorial direction generally sits in the low-to-mid lakhs per month, depending on output volume and how much of the team needs to be built.

The cheaper the engagement, the more you are buying execution rather than diagnosis. A retainer only makes sense if you are shipping enough volume for a system to matter.

How do I choose a content strategist for my YouTube channel or Instagram page?

Ask for the retention graphs, not the view counts. Anyone can show you a video that did well; a strategist should be able to show you why it held attention and what they changed to make that happen.

Three questions separate operators from consultants: what did you personally change in the edit, what did you stop making and why, and can you show a channel where the median video improved rather than just the best one.

What is a content audit and do I need one?

A content audit is a review of everything you have already published, scored on hook strength, retention shape and format performance, that tells you what to make more of and what to stop making entirely.

You need one if you are producing consistently but the results are inconsistent, because that pattern almost always means the channel has no repeatable format — not that you need to post more.
