About

Aditya Vora, content strategist

Aditya Vora is a content strategist and creative director based in Pune, India, who specialises in short-form video for YouTube and Instagram. He has spent six years as Short-Form Specialist at Brand Warikoo, Ankur Warikoo's 11.6M-subscriber channel, where he still leads short-form, and has shipped more than five billion views.

Pune, Maharashtra, India · Content strategy, scripting, editorial systems

What he does

Aditya Vora helps creators and founder-led brands turn content into a system instead of a guessing game. The work sits in three places: what the channel is actually about, what happens in the first three seconds, and whether the team can produce that repeatedly without him.

Most engagements start as a diagnosis. He reads the existing catalogue against hook strength, retention shape and format performance, then rebuilds the editorial system around whatever the data says is working. The output is not more content. It is usually less content, made to a repeatable spec.

He works with a small number of clients — one to two new retainers a quarter — across creator channels, founder personal brands, and companies building owned media. Reach him at adityavora16@gmail.com.

Background

Six years and counting as Short-Form Specialist at Brand Warikoo, working on scripting, editorial direction and the systems behind the channel's Shorts and Reels output. That is where the retention-first approach came from: at that volume, taste stops scaling and only a repeatable spec does.

Since then, work with 20+ creators and brands, including PR work on Taaza Khabar for BBKV and Bhuvan Bam, end-to-end content strategy for Autofy, and founder-brand content for Shawrya Mehrotra at Metvy. Along the way he built an independent practice past ₹50L in revenue.

He has taught short-form strategy and production at four schools: LIT School (short-form strategy and retention craft), IPLIX Media (editing direction and content systems), Terribly Tiny Tales (story and hook engineering), and WebVeda (editor SOPs and production workflow).
